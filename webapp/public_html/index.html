Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. There is nothing to pay, no deposit and no premium plan. It is a personal project built for learning, shared with anyone who finds it useful.

Is any real money involved?

No. The money is 100% virtual, orders are never sent to a real market, and there are no prizes, rankings with rewards or promotions.

Where do the prices come from?

From public data sources, updated four times a day on trading days (9:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 21:00, Rome time). They can be delayed or inaccurate, so this is a demo and not a source for real decisions.

What do I need to sign up?

A username, an email address and a password. You confirm your email and you are ready. You can delete your account and all your data at any time.

How do stop loss and take profit work here?

You set a price below (stop loss) and/or above (take profit) the current one. When a price update reaches that level, the whole position is sold. The execution price can differ from your level, as it can in real markets.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an educational simulation. Nothing on the site is a recommendation, and simulated results do not guarantee real-world results.
