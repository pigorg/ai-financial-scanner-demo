About this stock scanner

This free scanner ranks about 500 S&P 500 stocks by relative strength against the market, short-term momentum, trend (50 and 200-day moving averages, anchored VWAP) and earnings and revenue growth. Filter by sector, strength or growth, open a chart for any stock and export the results to CSV. The data is refreshed four times a day on weekdays.

Found an idea? Practise it risk-free: every ticker has Buy and Sell buttons that open the paper trading simulator, where you trade with virtual money. This is a demo for educational purposes, not financial advice.
